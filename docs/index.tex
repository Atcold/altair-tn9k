\section*{Stages}

\begin{description}
  \item[\href{running-leds.pdf}{Stage 0a: running LEDs, with Gowin EDA and the open-source flow}]
    The old LED project rebuilt in Gowin EDA, step by step; then the same two files through the open-source flow, from a \name{Makefile}.
  \item[\href{hello-analyser.pdf}{Stage 0b: hello world on the logic analyser}]
    A Verilog UART sends ‘Hello world’, read back by the logic analyser’s decoder and by a serial terminal.
  \item[\href{595-by-hand.pdf}{Stage 0c: one \name{74HC595} by hand}]
    Three buttons stand in for the FPGA: shift a byte in bit by bit, then latch it onto eight LEDs.
  \item[\href{595-walking-bit.pdf}{Stage 1a: a walking bit on the \name{74HC595}}]
    The FPGA takes the buttons’ place: one lit LED walks along the eight, a shift and a latch per tick.
\end{description}
